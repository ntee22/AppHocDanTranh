## English (en-US) — optional secondary listing

### Short description (≤ 80 characters)

```
Learn guzheng from scratch: lessons, sheet music and video guides by Đan Thanh
```

`77 characters.`

### Full description

```
Học Đàn Tranh is a guzheng (đàn tranh) learning app created by Đan Thanh — for complete beginners through to players who already know their way around the strings.

🎼 STRUCTURED LESSONS
Lessons in order, each with a video guide, a "what you'll learn" summary and practice tips. The app remembers which lessons you have finished, so you can pick up where you left off.

🎶 SHEET MUSIC LIBRARY
A hand-picked collection of guzheng sheet music compiled and arranged by Đan Thanh. Search by song title, and tap any page to zoom in for a closer look at the notation.

📺 VIDEO GUIDES
Song performances, tips and technique tutorials from Đan Thanh's channels.

🇻🇳 VIETNAMESE AND ENGLISH
Switch between Tiếng Việt and English at any time in Settings.

✨ QUIET BY DESIGN
No ads. No account required. No personal data collected.

ABOUT ĐAN THANH
Đan Thanh offers offline classes in Tân Bình and Gò Vấp, as well as online group classes and private 1-on-1 lessons. Feel free to reach out with any questions or feedback — thank you so much for your support!

Facebook: facebook.com/dantranhhcm
TikTok & YouTube: @danthanhdantranh
```
